How we make money

Marge Brief carries no advertising and no affiliate links. It is funded by its publisher, AlturaCom, which also makes Résidence Manager and Maya Pilot: if a reader finds one of these products here and chooses to buy it, the publisher benefits.

For that reason a product appears in an article only when the subject justifies it, with at most one link per article, flagged as “a tool we publish”. The content stays useful without it. If this changes (ads, affiliate links, sponsorship), we will write it here before we do it.
