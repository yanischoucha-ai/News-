US Short-Term Rental Rules: 4 Checks Before You Buy

A fallback stress test, an Oklahoma HOA ruling, pending city votes and the nightly cost of 2027 lodging taxes.

In short: At the November 19 average mortgage rate of 7.28%, our example property loses about $186 a month as a long-term rental. A 5-4 Oklahoma ruling shows covenant wording can end nightly stays. Check the fallback, the covenant, pending votes and tax changes before you make an offer.
Contents

Before buying a US short-term rental, check four things: whether it survives as a long-term rental, what the HOA covenant says, what your city is about to vote on, and what new lodging taxes do to your nightly rate. The first test got harder: the average 30-year mortgage rate reached 7.28% on November 19, 2026, against 6.26% a year earlier.

Key takeaways

  • 30-year fixed average: 7.28% on November 19, 2026, up from 7.11% a week earlier (Freddie Mac).
  • November 10, 2026: Oklahoma's Supreme Court upheld, 5-4, an HOA's use of a residential-purpose covenant to stop a short-term rental.
  • November 20, 2026: Airbnb's new house-rules policy took effect, with initial reviews running through March 2027.

Would the property survive as a long-term rental?

A local ban, a covenant lawsuit or a permit cap can remove nightly stays while the mortgage stays. So price the fallback first. The inputs below are an invented example, not market data: a $350,000 purchase, 25% down, a $262,500 loan over 30 years, $2,600 a month in long-term rent, 15% of rent set aside for vacancy and repairs, and $600 a month for property tax and insurance.

  1. Net before debt: $2,600 × 85% − $600 = $1,610 a month.
  2. Principal and interest at 7.28%: about $1,796.
  3. Gap: $1,610 − $1,796 = about −$186 a month.
  4. Coverage ratio: $1,610 / $1,796 = 0.90.
  5. Rent needed to break even: ($1,796 + $600) / 0.85 = about $2,819.

A negative result does not automatically kill a deal. It tells you what a rule change would cost: here, $186 a month, or $2,232 a year, out of pocket. Decide in advance how many months of that you can carry, and keep it in cash. For the short-term side of the model, run fees, occupancy and costs through the short-term rental profit calculator.

Does the HOA covenant actually allow nightly stays?

Read the recorded covenant itself, because a listing agent's "no rental restriction" can miss a business-use clause. On November 10, the Oklahoma Supreme Court ruled 5-4 against an Edmond owner in the Silverhawk neighborhood. The covenant allowed lots "for private residence purposes only" and barred maintaining a store or business there. The majority held that the rental was a commercial use.

The numbers in that case are public: the owner bought in 2015, and the evidence showed 36 stays and 141 guests between late 2021 and late 2023. The court affirmed an injunction and $8,528 in HOA assessments and penalties, and left an attorney fee award in place. The HOA had sued in November 2021.

The ruling turns on that wording and does not settle every Oklahoma HOA, much less other states. The four dissenting justices argued that a business ban did not clearly cover short stays. That split is the warning: if the highest court of a state can divide 5-4 on a sentence, ask a local real estate attorney to read yours before your inspection period closes. Search the documents for "residential purposes", "business", "commercial", "transient", "minimum lease term" and any board power to amend rental rules.

What is your city or county about to change?

A permit that exists today tells you little about next quarter. November alone produced moves in opposite directions.

Corpus Christi had also imposed a temporary pause on new permits of one type in August, and a full ban, a grandfathering option and the original ordinance all remained possible when the council postponed. Clark County shows how slow the earlier path was: FOX5 reported 830 complete applications three years earlier, 242 approved licenses and 47 pending.

Ask the clerk three questions in writing: is a permit transferable on sale, is any ordinance pending, and what happens to existing permits if it passes. Never assume the seller's permit follows the deed.

How much do new lodging taxes cost per night?

If you keep the guest's total price flat, a higher tax comes out of your rate. The formula is: new pre-tax rate = old rate × (1 + old tax) / (1 + new tax). Use your full combined tax rate when you have it. The example isolates the local tax that is changing, with an invented $200 nightly rate and 150 booked nights.

Collier County voters approved the one-point increase, which covers stays of six months or less including vacation rentals. Tulsa voters approved the hike with about 57% support, but it applies to short-term rentals with five or more rooms, so a one-bedroom unit is outside that example. The other option is to raise the guest price and watch occupancy. Either way, update the model before you make an offer, and confirm who remits each tax: the platform may collect some and leave others to you.

What changed on November 20, and what should you do this week?

Airbnb's new house-rules policy took effect on November 20. The company says initial reviews run through March 2027 and that hosts get at least 20 days to fix a rule or appeal. Separate guest contracts are allowed only for limited reasons, including legal or insurance requirements, HOA rules, monthly-stay agreements, Luxe listings and optional amenities such as golf carts. Hosts cannot require guests to wash dishes, strip beds or take trash off-site, and generally should not impose fines. A local ordinance or HOA rule can be uploaded in an appeal.

That matters for the checks above: if your city or association requires a clause, keep the exact text and the document that requires it, ready to upload.

  • Compare each house rule with the new policy and delete chores it no longer allows.
  • Save the ordinance or covenant behind every rule you keep.
  • Move cleaning tasks into the turnover plan; our guide to self-managing versus hiring a property manager prices the hours.
  • Recheck your guest agreement against the permitted reasons.

Keep access codes, WiFi and the house guide out of the rules list as well: arrival details are easier to maintain in a message template or in a guest portal opened by QR code. Neither one replaces the house rules on your listing or any permit requirement.

The pre-offer checklist to copy

1. Long-term rent from signed comparables: $____. Quoted rate: ____%. Monthly gap: $____. Months of gap held in cash: ____.
2. Recorded covenants read; wording on business, residential use, minimum term: ____. Attorney review date: ____.
3. Permit: transferable? ____ Pending ordinance or moratorium? ____ Next council date: ____.
4. Combined lodging tax now: ____%. Announced change and date: ____. Who remits: ____.
5. Offer contingency: closing depends on written confirmation of items 2 and 3.

Send this to the clerk before your contingency deadline: "I am considering buying [address]. Is short-term rental use allowed in this zone, does an existing permit transfer to a new owner, and is any change scheduled for a vote? Please point me to the ordinance."

When should you walk away?

Walk away, or reprice, when the long-term fallback loses more than you can fund for your chosen number of months, when the covenant wording is unclear and nobody will confirm it before closing, or when a pending vote could end the use you are paying for. These checks matter less for a cash buyer who is content with long-term rent, or for an owner-occupied room rental governed by different local rules, though both should still read the covenant. They do not cover federal income tax, financing eligibility or insurance. Frequently asked questions

Can an HOA ban Airbnb-style rentals?

It depends on the recorded wording and state law. Oklahoma's Supreme Court ruled 5-4 on November 10, 2026 that one neighborhood's residential-purpose and no-business covenant barred a short-term rental, without deciding every covenant.

What mortgage rate should I use to test a rental purchase?

Use your written lender quote. The Freddie Mac average was 7.28% for a 30-year fixed loan on November 19, 2026, but its survey reflects owner-occupied loans with 20% down.

How do I price a higher lodging tax without raising the guest total?

Multiply your pre-tax rate by one plus the old tax, then divide by one plus the new tax. On a $200 night, moving from 5% to 6% leaves about $198.11.

What did Airbnb change about house rules in November 2026?

A new policy took effect on November 20, 2026 that limits chores, fines and separate guest contracts. Airbnb says hosts get at least 20 days to fix a flagged rule or appeal, and initial reviews run through March 2027.
