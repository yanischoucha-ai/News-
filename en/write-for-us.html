Write for Marge

You run rentals, job sites, an agency, an AI project? You’ve seen what works and what doesn’t. We publish every day in English and French, and we’re looking for authors who write about what they actually do.

What we publish

  • Real cases with numbers: revenue, margins, lead times, costs, before and after.
  • Analysis that challenges received wisdom, backed by data or verifiable experience.
  • Methods you applied yourself, including what went wrong.

What we turn down

  • Generic content anyone could have signed.
  • Disguised sponsored posts and undisclosed affiliate links.
  • “10 tips” lists with no case or data.

How it works

  1. You send the topic and angle, or the full article.
  2. We read every pitch and reply by email.
  3. We edit together: number checks, structure, headline.
  4. We publish with your name, a short bio and a link to your site or profile.

Terms

You remain the owner of your text. You grant us a non-exclusive licence to publish it on Marge, in English and French (translation included). The text must be unpublished. One promotional link to your business is accepted and is presented as such.
