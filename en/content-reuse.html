Quoting and reusing our articles

The texts, tables, calculations, diagrams and headlines published on Marge Brief are protected by copyright. Here is what you can do freely, and what needs our permission.

What you can do freely

  • Quote a short excerpt (150 words maximum) to illustrate or comment on your point, naming Marge Brief as the source and linking to the original article.
  • Share an article’s link, or summarize it in your own words while crediting the source.
  • Use a figure by pointing to its original source, listed in the article, and to our article if you rely on our analysis.

What needs our written permission

  • Republishing an article, in full or in large part, including translated or rewritten from ours.
  • Feeding a website, newsletter or commercial content with our texts.
  • Reproducing our tables, calculations or diagrams without credit.
  • Training an AI model on our content. Assistants and search engines that cite our articles with a link to the source are welcome.

How to credit us
