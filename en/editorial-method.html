How we work

Marge Brief publishes sourced articles every day for short-term rental hosts, tradespeople and independents. This page says who publishes it, how, under which rules, and how to correct us.

Who publishes

Marge Brief is published by AlturaCom, a digital communication agency. The publisher also develops other services, such as Résidence Manager (a tool for hosts) and Maya Pilot (a marketing tool for software companies). When an article points to one of them, we say so in the text: it is never presented as an independent review.

How articles are made

Articles are written with the help of artificial intelligence (Claude, by Anthropic) from research on identified sources, then published automatically under written quality rules. We do not claim they are handwritten, and we never invent a fictional author or personal experience for them.

Rules every article must meet

  • At least three sources, including a primary source (legislation, a public agency, the original study), listed at the bottom of the article with links.
  • A calculation or numerical test you can redo with your own numbers, with the assumptions shown.
  • A visible verification date. When something changes, the article is corrected and the date updated.
  • Limits stated: when existing figures come from tool vendors or weak studies, we say so.
  • No invented figure, testimonial, price or experience. No promise of income or results.
  • If a day does not yield a useful new fact or calculation, we publish less rather than fill space.

What we are not

Our articles inform; they do not replace legal, tax, accounting or financial advice. For a binding decision, check with a professional and with the official source cited.

Guest texts

The Community section hosts texts written by readers. They are published immediately after automated checks, then reviewed afterwards; we remove those that break our rules. They represent their authors, not the editorial team.

Report an error

An error, an outdated figure, a source that does not say what we wrote? Report it through the Citing and reusing our articles page, which explains how to contact us. We check and correct, and we date the correction.
