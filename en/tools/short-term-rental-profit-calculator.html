How it is calculated

  • Nights booked = 365 × occupancy. Stays = nights booked ÷ average stay.
  • Gross revenue = nights booked × rate + stays × cleaning fee charged.
  • Net = gross − platform fee − stays × cleaning cost − 12 × fixed costs.
  • Net yield = annual net ÷ capital invested.

Limits

Does not compute income tax or depreciation, and occupancy taxes and local lodging taxes must be added to your costs. The example values are not market averages.
