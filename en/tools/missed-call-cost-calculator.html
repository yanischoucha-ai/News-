How it is calculated

  • Lost missed calls = calls × share missed × share who go elsewhere.
  • Jobs lost = lost missed calls × close rate.
  • Revenue lost = jobs lost × average job value; margin lost = revenue lost × margin.
  • Cost of one missed call = close rate × average job × share who go elsewhere × margin.

Limits

Each rate is an estimate you should measure yourself (call log, signed quotes). An answering service or tool has a cost too: compare its monthly price to the margin lost.
