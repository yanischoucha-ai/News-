How it is calculated

  • CAC = spend ÷ new customers.
  • Payback = CAC ÷ (monthly revenue × gross margin).
  • LTV = monthly revenue × gross margin ÷ monthly churn.
  • LTV/CAC = LTV ÷ CAC.

Limits

Assumes constant churn and ignores expansion revenue. With few customers the figures swing month to month: use an average over several months.
