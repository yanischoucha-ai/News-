About Marge Brief

Marge Brief is an online publication, in French for France and in English for the United States, for people who run a small business. Our goal: you leave with a calculation, a source or a method you can use this week, whether or not you ever buy anything.

Three subjects, nothing else

  • Hosts and short-term rentals: operations, net profitability, regulation, taxes, rental investing.
  • Contractors and small service businesses: missed calls, quotes, pricing, visibility, reviews, business structure, cash flow.
  • SaaS marketing and growth: content, community management, social media, algorithms.

Who publishes this site

Marge Brief is published by AlturaCom, which also makes products:

  • Résidence Manager: a platform for hosts and property managers (QR-code guest portal, cleaning, on-demand services).
  • AlturaCom: a digital communications agency for tradespeople and small service businesses (websites, content, video, acquisition and SEO). Its website is currently in French.
  • Maya Pilot: an AI community manager for SaaS companies.

These products do not have a customer base yet, so we show no clients, no figures from results and no testimonials. When an article mentions one, it says so in the sentence (“a tool we publish”) and never presents it as an independent review. Details: how we make money.
